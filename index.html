About

PhD candidate at the University of Innsbruck (Department of Microbiology) and research associate at MCI | The Entrepreneurial School, Innsbruck. My research focuses on bio-based gasification chars and activated carbon in wastewater treatment and anaerobic digestion. The project is funded by the Christian Doppler Research Association (CDG).
